## About the game

Pokémon TRAILS is a fan-made browser adventure developed by JellyMudkip. Choose a trainer and a first Pokémon, then explore an endless, randomly generated region filled with routes and battles. This edition runs the game locally on your Windows computer, so you can play without the online services.
The game run online during 2022, but it reached the end due to time-managment, server administration issues and more. Now, you can play the local version in your browser.

## Features

- Explore a procedurally varied adventure and take on Pokémon battles.
- Choose your trainer and starting Pokémon.
- Beat 8 Pokémon gyms.
- Manage your team and collection with the Pokédex and PC.
- Challenge the Battle Tower.
- Play in English or Spanish.
- Keep your save data on your own computer.
- Start playing without installing XAMPP or configuring a database.

The offline edition disables online features such as chat, energy, pokemon league, player search, and rankings.

## Download and play

1. Download the [Windows installer](https://github.com/PokemonTrails/PokemonTrailsOffline/releases/PokemonTrails-Setup.exe).
2. Run `PokemonTrails-Setup.exe` and choose English or Spanish, or extract the ZIP to a folder of your choice.
3. Start `PokemonTrails.exe`. On the first launch, choose a player name to create your local save.

Your game data is stored in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PokemonTrails`. The portable version does not require XAMPP on your computer. For save locations, launch options, and build instructions, see [`LOCAL-WINDOWS.md`](LOCAL-WINDOWS.md).

## Languages

The installer asks which language to use when the game starts for the first time. You can also change the language in the game.
